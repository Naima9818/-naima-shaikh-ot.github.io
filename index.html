About

Therapy designed around real life.

Occupational therapy focuses on helping people participate in the everyday activities that give life meaning — self-care, school, work, play, mobility, routines and community participation.

Home-based therapy allows assessment and intervention in the environment where daily challenges actually happen. Sessions can be tailored to the person's goals, abilities, routines and family context.

My approach: functional, person-centred and goal-oriented, with caregiver education and practical strategies wherever appropriate.

Person-centred goalsTherapy begins with what the individual wants and needs to do.
Functional practiceSkills are connected to meaningful everyday occupations.
Family & caregiver supportEducation and home strategies to encourage carryover between sessions.
